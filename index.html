About

Regal by nature.
Wild by choice.

Based in Ayia Napa, Cyprus, Adele Moon is a DJ moving freely between afrobeats, amapiano and afro house. Her sets blend log drum grooves and live percussion with warm, hypnotic melodies — from sunset sessions on the beach to packed late-night dancefloors.

She plays regularly at Tulum Beach Bar, Nissi Bay Beach Bar and Luna Park in Ayia Napa, shares her live sets with a growing online following, and is available for international bookings.
