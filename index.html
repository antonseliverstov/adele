About

Regal by nature.
Wild by choice.

Based between Berlin and Cape Town, Adele Moon is a DJ and producer moving freely between afrobeats, amapiano and afro house. Her sets blend log drum grooves and live percussion with warm, hypnotic melodies built for late-night dancefloors.

She has played clubs and festivals across Europe and South Africa, and regularly shares new mixes with her growing online following.
